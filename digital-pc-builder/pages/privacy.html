Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Digital PC Builder is an academic project. This policy explains what data the system stores, where it is kept and how you can remove it. It does not have user accounts, sign-in, advertising or analytics.

1. Data we store

  • Your current build: the parts you selected, build name, budget, intended use, preferred resolution and notes.
  • Saved builds: the same information for every build you choose to save.
  • Comparison list: the IDs of components you ticked for comparison in the catalog.

We do not ask for, collect or store names, email addresses, passwords, payment details or location.

2. Where the data is kept

  • In your browser: the current build and comparison list are always kept in your browser's localStorage on your own device. When the site runs without its PHP backend, saved builds are kept there too.
  • On the server: when the site runs with its PHP and MySQL backend (for example on XAMPP), saved builds are written to the builds table of the database run by whoever hosts the site. Anyone who can use that copy of the site can see and change those saved builds.

3. Cookies and tracking

The system does not set cookies and does not use tracking, advertising or analytics tools.

4. Third-party services

Pages with charts load the Chart.js library from the jsDelivr content delivery network (cdn.jsdelivr.net). Like any web request, this shares your IP address and browser details with that provider. If the library cannot be loaded, the charts are drawn without it.

5. Exported files

When you export a build as JSON or CSV, or print it, the file is created in your browser and saved to your device. It is not uploaded anywhere.

6. Removing your data

  • Delete individual saved builds from the Saved Builds page.
  • Use Clear in the PC Builder to empty the current build.
  • Clear this site's data in your browser settings to remove everything stored on your device.

7. Changes to this policy

If the way the system handles data changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the project team through the course or institution where the project is presented.
